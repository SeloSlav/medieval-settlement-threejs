Villager model assets
=====================

The two quaternius-villager GLB files in this directory are Quaternius models
distributed under Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Assets and source pages:

- quaternius-villager-man.glb
  "Man" by Quaternius, from the Animated Men Pack
  https://poly.pizza/m/fjHyMd5Wxw
  https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Men-Pack-DAC9SDgMQT
  Downloaded GLB:
  https://static.poly.pizza/985eacbf-9dde-44b7-9270-4e35c8400b13.glb

- quaternius-villager-woman.glb
  "Woman in Dress" by Quaternius, from the Animated Women Pack
  https://poly.pizza/m/zMyPlQXBzq
  https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Women-Pack-HHSKxnk1mY
  Downloaded GLB:
  https://static.poly.pizza/a642af96-e239-4c5f-b50d-7661ff51deec.glb

- worker-male-common-01-v002.glb
  Project-supplied Tripo-generated worker asset. This file is not covered by
  the Quaternius CC0 notice above. Its animation-index provenance and the
  SHA-256 of the original export are retained in
  worker-male-common-01-v002.animation-map.json.

- worker-female-common-01-v001.glb
  Project-supplied Tripo-generated worker asset. This file is not covered by
  the Quaternius CC0 notice above. Its animation-index provenance and the
  SHA-256 of the original export are retained in
  worker-female-common-01-v001.animation-map.json.

- cleric-monk-common-01-v001.glb
  Project-supplied Tripo-generated medieval monk asset used by chapel priests
  and monastery monks. This file is not covered by the Quaternius CC0 notice
  above. Its complete 20-clip semantic animation manifest and source SHA-256
  are retained in cleric-monk-common-01-v001.animation-map.json.

- ottoman-raider-common-01-v001.glb
  Project-supplied Tripo-generated medieval warrior asset used exclusively by
  Ottoman frontier raiders. This file is not covered by the Quaternius CC0
  notice above. Its complete 14-clip semantic animation manifest and original
  export SHA-256 are retained in
  ottoman-raider-common-01-v001.animation-map.json.

The Quaternius models retain their authored rigs and idle/walk animation clips.
Attribution is not required by CC0, but this record is kept with the assets so
their provenance and reuse terms remain auditable.
