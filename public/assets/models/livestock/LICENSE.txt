Livestock model assets
======================

The GLB files in this directory are Quaternius models distributed under
Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Assets and source pages:

- quaternius-cow.glb
  "Cow" by Quaternius
  https://poly.pizza/m/26zM1outCr

- quaternius-bull.glb
  "Bull" by Quaternius
  https://poly.pizza/m/a8PIIYwF7r

- quaternius-sheep.glb
  "Sheep" by Quaternius
  Original Sheep.fbx from the official Farm Animal Pack download:
  https://quaternius.com/packs/farmanimal.html

- quaternius-goat.glb
  Blender-authored goat derivative using the Quaternius Sheep body and the
  original Quaternius Cow horn geometry and material listed above.

- quaternius-pig.glb
  "Pig" by Quaternius
  Original Pig.fbx from the official Farm Animal Pack download:
  https://quaternius.com/packs/farmanimal.html

- quaternius-chicken.glb
  "Chicken" by Quaternius
  https://poly.pizza/m/ineV9pU5VL

The pig and sheep GLBs were converted in Blender from the official FBX files.
Their authored Idle and Jump actions are preserved. Idle-derived Walk and
Idle_Eating action aliases keep the game's livestock motion-state contract
without altering the source rig or mesh.

Their eyes reuse the original Quaternius cow's low-poly `Eye_Black` geometry,
triangular `Eye_White` catchlights, and material values. The copied eye facets
are fitted to each head in Blender and weighted to the existing Head bone, so
they follow every authored animation. The sheep-derived goat inherits the same
eye geometry and preserves its black-and-white palette at runtime.

The backyard goat visual uses `quaternius-goat.glb`. Blender fits the original
cow's faceted `Horns` meshes and material to the Sheep skull and weights every
horn vertex to the existing Head bone. The game applies a leaner scale,
goat-colored body-material clones, and a procedural beard while preserving the
authored horn and eye palettes. The published Farm Animal Pack does not include
a separate goat file; this derivative keeps the requested pack provenance and
CC0 terms without importing a differently licensed model.
