Livestock model assets
======================

The GLB files in this directory are Quaternius models distributed under
Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Assets and source pages:

- quaternius-pig.glb

- quaternius-chicken.glb
  "Chicken" by Quaternius
  https://poly.pizza/m/ineV9pU5VL

The backyard goat visual reuses `quaternius-sheep.glb` from the same CC0 Farm
Animal Pack. The game creates the goat variant at runtime with a leaner scale,
goat-colored material clones, and procedural horns and beard while retaining
the source rig and animations. The published Farm Animal Pack does not include
a separate goat file; this derivative keeps the requested pack provenance and
CC0 terms without importing a differently licensed model.

Collection pages:

- Farm Animal Pack: https://quaternius.com/packs/farmanimal.html
- Animated Animal Pack: https://poly.pizza/bundle/Animated-Animal-Pack-ILAPXeUYiS

The original author does not require attribution under CC0. This file is kept
with the game so that the provenance and reuse terms remain easy to audit.
