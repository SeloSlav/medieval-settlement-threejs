Worker tool model assets
========================

The GLB files and shared colormap texture in this directory are from Kenney's
Survival Kit 2.0, distributed under Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal
(CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Creator and source:

- Kenney (https://kenney.nl/)
- Survival Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/survival-kit)
- Release date: 2024-04-03

Files used by this project:

- kenney-tool-hatchet.glb
  Renamed from `Models/GLB format/tool-axe.glb`.
- kenney-tool-pickaxe.glb
  Renamed from `Models/GLB format/tool-pickaxe.glb`.
- kenney-tool-hammer.glb
  Renamed from `Models/GLB format/tool-hammer.glb`.
- kenney-tool-hoe.glb
  Renamed from `Models/GLB format/tool-hoe.glb`.
- kenney-tool-shovel.glb
  Renamed from `Models/GLB format/tool-shovel.glb`.
- Textures/colormap.png
  Shared palette texture referenced by the GLB files.

Kenney permits personal, educational, and commercial use. Attribution is not
required by CC0, but this record is kept with the assets so their provenance
and reuse terms remain auditable.

Frontier spear
--------------

`quaternius-spear.glb` is the low-poly Spear by Quaternius, downloaded from
Poly Pizza and distributed under Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal
(CC0 1.0):

- Creator: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/)
- Asset page: https://poly.pizza/m/3zA9NtYBEi
- Direct GLB: https://static.poly.pizza/6a63e2ad-5260-4ace-9245-7bfffdfd9695.glb

The spear remains unmodified at source; the game scales and attaches it to the
existing Quaternius villager rig at runtime.
