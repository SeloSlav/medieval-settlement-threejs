# Generation prompt set

Mode: built-in image generator. Each icon was generated separately as a new
raster asset, then downsampled to a 512 × 512 RGBA PNG for runtime use.

Across the five prompts, the shared brief was:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: development-tree game UI icon. Style:
> richly painted, semi-realistic medieval game icon with finely inked contours
> and tactile natural materials; match a premium isolated resource-icon set,
> not a manuscript page or building illustration. Composition: centered compact
> emblem on a square canvas, strong at 64 pixels, fully visible with generous
> even padding. Historically plausible for inland Gorski Kotar around
> 1500–1550. Genuinely transparent alpha background with no backdrop. Avoid
> parchment, paper, rectangular cards, borders, frames, badges, circles,
> shields, buildings, landscapes, ground shadows, text, letters, numerals,
> logos, and watermarks.

The icon-specific requests were:

- **Carters’ Guild:** one sturdy sixteenth-century wooden cart wheel with an
  iron tyre, crossed behind a compact wooden carter’s mallet and a short coil of
  hemp rope; tactile oak, dark forged iron, and coarse rope.
- **Chartered Markets:** a compact sixteenth-century merchant balance with two
  shallow brass pans, backed by one tightly rolled market charter tied with
  cord and closed by a plain dark-red wax seal; no visible writing.
- **Watch Fires:** one compact iron beacon basket filled with split wood and a
  controlled orange flame, with a small unlit hand bell hanging from its side;
  dark forged iron and charred oak; no tower, architecture, or horn.
- **Coppice Craft:** a low-cut hazel stool sending up several fresh green shoots,
  crossed with a compact iron billhook, with three harvested straight poles
  bound beside it by a thin hemp withe; no conifer, pine cone, or modern saw.
- **River Wardens:** one native river trout curving around a small hand-woven
  willow fish trap, with three simple blue-gray water ripples beneath; no camp,
  net backdrop, modern equipment, or water scene.
